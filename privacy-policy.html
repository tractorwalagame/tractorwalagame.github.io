Privacy Policy

This website is built to collect as little information as possible. This page explains what that means in practice.

Summary

Tractor Wala Game is a static website. It has no user accounts, no forms that collect personal data, no comments and no database. The site's own pages do not set cookies and do not load third-party analytics or advertising scripts.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver pages and protect its service. We do not control that processing. See GitHub's own privacy statement for details.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the site. If you contact us through GitHub, your message and your GitHub profile are handled by GitHub under its terms and may be public.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not use cookies. It does not store personal data in your browser.

Analytics and search tools

At the time of writing no analytics tool is installed. If the owner later adds one, for example Google Search Console verification or an analytics service, this policy must be updated first.

External links

Pages may link to other websites, such as official app stores. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

Children

The site is a general information resource and is not directed at collecting data from children. If children play games discussed here, parents should check the age rating and privacy information on each game's official listing.

Changes

This policy may change if the site changes. The current version applies to the pages you are reading.
