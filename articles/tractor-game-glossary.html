Tractor Game Glossary: Game Terms and Hindi Search Phrases

Game menus and search phrases use terms that new players may not know. This glossary explains them in plain language, including common Hindi words used in searches.

Hindi and Hinglish search phrases

  • Wala, wali, wale: "the one with" or "related to". A tractor wala game is a game with tractors.
  • Ka: "of". Tractor ka game means a tractor game.
  • Trali / trolley: the trailer pulled by a tractor. See Tractor Trolley Wala Game.
  • Dikhao, dikhaiye: "show me". See Tractor Wala Game Dikhao and Tractor Wala Game Dikhaiye.
  • Chahie: "I need" or "I want". See Tractor Wala Game Chahie.
  • Download karo: "download it". See Tractor Wala Game Download.

Game terms

  • 3D: graphics with depth, so you can see a scene from different angles. See Tractor Games 3D.
  • Simulator: a game that tries to imitate real machine behaviour. See Tractor Simulator Game.
  • Mission: a task with a goal, such as ploughing a field.
  • Free drive: a mode without goals for practice.
  • Implement: equipment attached to a tractor, such as a plough or seeder.
  • HUD: heads-up display, the information on the screen during play.
  • Camera view: the viewing position, such as cabin, chase or top view.
  • Sensitivity: how strongly the game reacts to steering input.

Technical terms

  • APK: Android installation file. See Tractor Wala Game APK Guide.
  • RAM: short-term memory used by apps while they run.
  • Storage: space for installed apps and files.
  • FPS: frames per second. Higher numbers feel smoother.
  • Lag: delay or stutter in response.
  • Cache: temporary data that apps store to load faster.
  • Permissions: access an app requests, such as storage or location.
  • Developer: the person or company that makes the game.

Money terms

  • In-app purchase: paying real money inside the game.
  • Ads: advertisements shown during play.
  • Freemium: free to start, with optional paid items.

Safety terms

  • Official store: the app store provided for your device.
  • Sideloading: installing an app from outside the store.
  • Malware: harmful software.

How to use this glossary

When you meet an unfamiliar term in a store listing or guide, return here. If you want to see the terms in practice, begin with Tractor Wala Game for Beginners.

Frequently asked questions

Is wala different from wale?

No. They are grammatical forms of the same word and are used interchangeably in searches.

What does FPS mean?

Frames per second, a measure of how smoothly the game displays.

Is sideloading safe?

It carries more risk than installing from an official store. See the APK guide.
