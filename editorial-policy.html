Editorial Policy and Corrections Process

These are the standards we follow when we write and update guides, and the process for correcting mistakes.

Our standards

  1. Be useful first. Pages exist to answer a reader's question, not to repeat a keyword.
  2. Be accurate. We do not state game details we cannot verify. When details are unknown, we tell you what to check.
  3. Be honest about limits. We have not tested every game and do not claim to.
  4. Attribute sources. External facts are attributed to their source, and we avoid copying text from others.
  5. No fabrication. We do not publish fake reviews, ratings, player counts, testimonials or author credentials.

Downloads and games

We do not provide download links to game files, and we do not describe a game as free or available unless that has been verified from an official source at the time. Verified information, when added, is dated.

Images and video

Illustrations on this site are generic artwork and are labelled as such. They are not screenshots of any game. We embed a video only after verifying its source and that embedding is permitted.

Commercial relationships

We currently accept no payment for coverage and display no advertising or affiliate links. Any change will be disclosed on the pages affected.

Corrections process

  1. A reader reports an issue through the Contact page.
  2. We review the claim against official sources.
  3. If the page is wrong, we correct it and note the change where it matters.
  4. If we cannot verify a claim either way, we remove or soften it.

Updates

Guides are revised when information changes or when a reader points out a problem. We do not change a page's date or content solely to appear fresh.
