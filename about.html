About Tractor Wala Game

Tractor Wala Game is an independent information website about tractor games. This page explains what we do, what we do not do and how to reach us.

What this site is

Tractor Wala Game is a static guide website about tractor simulators, 3D farming games, Indian tractor games and trolley games. It explains how to find, evaluate, install and play them, with a focus on safe sources and honest information. The site's home is the homepage.

What we do

  • Write original guides to genres, features and common problems
  • Explain how to check developers, permissions and device compatibility
  • Provide checklists and comparison methods instead of unverified rankings

What we do not do

  • We do not host, distribute or link to game files
  • We do not claim any game is free, available or safe unless that has been verified
  • We do not publish fake reviews, ratings or testimonials
  • We do not claim affiliation with any game developer

Who writes the content

The site is published under the name Tractor Wala Game. Individual author profiles have not been published, and we do not list credentials we cannot document. Our standards are described in the Editorial Policy.

Independence and trademarks

Game names and brands mentioned belong to their owners. Mentions are for identification only and do not imply endorsement.

Contact

Corrections, suggestions and questions are welcome through the Contact page.
